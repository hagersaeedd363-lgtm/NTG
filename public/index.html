Pro Tips

Level up faster

Small habits that compound into a stronger squad and a healthier coin balance.

01

Chase chemistry, not just OVR

A linked 84-rated side often beats a mismatched 90 one. Prioritise same-league and same-nation cores early.

02

Never let energy sit full

Energy caps out and wastes regen. Burn it on Live Events or Skill Games before you log off.

03

Sell duplicates, don't hoard

Untradeable dupes feed Skill Boosts and training; tradeable ones fund your next big pull on the market.

04

Master the skill moves

Two or three reliable moves — a quick roll, a ball-roll, a feint — beat spamming flashy combos you can't land.

05

Defend with positioning

Hold the line and jockey. Auto-tackle spam gets punished online — contain, then strike on the loose ball.

06

Do the daily grind

Login rewards, daily objectives and a couple of VS Attack runs stack up huge over a full season.

FAQ

The quick answers new managers ask most.

Is FC Mobile free to play?

Yes. EA SPORTS FC Mobile is free to download and play on iOS and Android. Optional in-app purchases exist, but a competitive squad is fully achievable through play.

What phones can run it?

Most modern iOS and Android devices from the last few years handle it comfortably. A stable connection matters most for online modes like Head to Head.

How is it different from FIFA Mobile?

FC Mobile is the successor to FIFA Mobile following EA's rebrand to EA SPORTS FC. It carries the same core loop with updated engines, licenses, presentation and seasonal content.

What is chemistry and why does it matter?

Chemistry rewards squads whose players share leagues, nations or clubs. Higher chemistry boosts in-match attributes, so a well-linked team punches above its raw rating.

How do I get better players for free?

Grind Live Events, complete objectives, climb Division Rivals for rank rewards, and reinvest market profits. Patience and consistency beat one-off spending.
